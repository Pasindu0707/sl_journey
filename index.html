Find Your Perfect Experience

Things to do in Sri Lanka

An island packed into one — wildlife, heritage, mountains and ocean, all within a few hours' drive.

Wildlife

Safari in Yala

Spot leopards, elephants and a wealth of wildlife in Sri Lanka's most iconic national park.

Heritage

Cultural Exploration

Discover the island's soul at Sigiriya, Anuradhapura and the sacred Temple of the Tooth in Kandy.

Trekking

Hiking & Trekking

Conquer Ella Rock, Adam's Peak and the wild ridges of the Knuckles Mountain Range.

Ocean

Diving & Surfing

Dive coral reefs and shipwrecks, then surf world-class breaks at Arugam Bay and Hikkaduwa.

Adventure

White-Water Rafting

Ride the Kelani River rapids in Kitulgala — the island's ultimate water adventure.

Scenic

Train Journeys

The Kandy–Ella ride is one of the world's most beautiful, crossing the iconic Nine Arch Bridge.

Nature

Rainforest Trails

Explore Sinharaja, a UNESCO rainforest brimming with endemic birds, life and ancient calm.

Marine

Whale Watching

See blue whales, sperm whales and dolphins off Mirissa — at their best from November to April.
